### 3D Printed Components & Attachments

During the development of the vehicle, structural space constraints on the stock chassis made it unfeasible to mount all electronic and mechanical components directly to the base plate. To maximize spatial efficiency and maintain a low center of gravity, we designed and 3D-printed several custom modular attachments. These components extend the chassis vertically and provide secure, vibration-resistant housings for our specialized hardware.
